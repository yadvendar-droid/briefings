arXiv:2610.14472v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Trapped ion quantum computing based on the quantum charge coupled device (QCCD) architecture uses functional zoning and ion transport to enable high fidelity computation and all to all qubit connectivity. As trapped ion quantum processing units (QPU) scale, 90{\deg} X-junction turns account for an increasing fraction of total transport time. Frequent junction crossings, therefore, make transport speed and final phonon excitation direct determinants of quantum algorithm execution efficiency. However, complex electric fields in X-junctions complicate waveform design, making it difficult to achieve high speed and low final phonon excitation simultaneously.
